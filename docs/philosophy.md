## 🎓 Why This Project Exists

This is a **learning project**. A free giveaway from Creators Eye.

We started from scratch. v1.0 was simple. v2.0 became professional.

**Why?**  
To show that **anyone can start small and grow**.

**What you learn here:**  
Windows Forms, SendInput API, hotkeys, mistakes simulation, human-like timing, borderless UI – one step at a time.

**What this is NOT:**  
This is not our premium work. This is the **free version** – limited hints of what we really build.

Our premium tools are **enterprise and military grade**. Used by teams in labs and research environments. Full ecosystems. CLI, API, plugins, hardware integration.

**But this free project?**  
This is for **you**. To learn. To practice. To build your own ideas.

**Start from scratch. Reach advanced.**

Free. Open. For learning.
